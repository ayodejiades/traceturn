## Description
traceturn — slopvestigation forensics for autonomous agent swarms. Ingests massive multi-agent JSONL transcripts (AI Village >170k turns, Hugging Face incident, collusion.wiki) and builds two interlocking deterministic graphs: a Causal Blame DAG that isolates the exact turn where a failure originated, and a Claim Lineage graph that traces how a single unverified premise propagated until it became swarm consensus with zero independent corroboration. Runs a standardized 5-probe investigator audit in under 2 seconds with zero LLM hallucinations in the attribution kernel.
