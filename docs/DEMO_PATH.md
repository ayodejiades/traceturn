# Demo path

1. **Welcome to the overview** — go to `/`
2. **Launch the live demo workspace** — click `[data-demo='launch-demo']`, wait for `[data-demo='list']`
3. **Create a new record** — click `[data-demo='new']`, fill `[data-demo='title']` with "Lagos pilot"
4. **Submit and verify real-time state** — click `[data-demo='submit']`, wait for `[data-demo='row']`

Everything not listed above is stubbed, hardcoded, or deleted. That is the correct prioritisation under a deadline, not technical debt.

## Fallback
